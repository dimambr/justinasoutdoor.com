Common Questions

FAQ

Частые вопросы
Will pressure washing damage my siding?
No. For siding, roofs, and delicate surfaces I use soft wash — low pressure (under 100 PSI) combined with biodegradable soaps that safely remove algae, mold, and dirt without stripping paint or damaging materials.
Нет. Для фасадов использую soft wash — низкое давление и биоразлагаемые моющие средства, которые безопасно удаляют грязь, не повреждая материал.
Do I need to be home during the service?
No. As long as I have access to an outdoor water spigot and the areas to be cleaned, you can be at work or running errands. I'll text you when I'm done.
Нет. Нужен только доступ к воде снаружи. Напишу вам когда закончу.
How long does it take?
Most services take 1–3 hours depending on the size of the home. Larger homes or full exterior packages may take longer.
1–3 часа в зависимости от размера дома. Большие дома или полный комплекс — дольше.
What chemicals do you use? Are they safe for plants and pets?
I use professional-grade cleaning solutions designed specifically for exterior surfaces — diluted sodium hypochlorite for soft washing and surfactants to lift dirt. I rinse landscaping before and after the job to protect plants. Pets should be kept inside during the service as a precaution.
Использую профессиональные моющие средства для наружных поверхностей. Поливаю растения до и после работы. Животных лучше держать в доме во время мойки.
Is your business insured?
Yes — fully insured with $1M/$2M general liability through Coterie/Progressive. Certificate of insurance available on request.
Да — страховка $1M/$2M через Coterie/Progressive. Сертификат страхования предоставлю по запросу.
What's your service area?
Twin Cities North & East Metro — within about 15 miles of Little Canada: Shoreview, Roseville, Maplewood, Vadnais Heights, North Oaks, Woodbury, and surrounding suburbs.
Twin Cities, в радиусе примерно 15 миль от Little Canada.
